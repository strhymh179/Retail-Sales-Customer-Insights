# Retail Sales & Customer Insights Dashboard

📌 **Project Overview**

This project analyzes retail sales performance and customer purchasing behavior using Power BI. The goal is to identify revenue trends, understand customer demographics, compare product category performance, and generate actionable insights to support data-driven business decisions.

## 🛠️ Project Workflow

### 🔹 Steps in Project

- **Requirement Gathering** – Define business objectives and identify key questions related to retail sales performance and customer behavior.
- **Data Collection** – Import the retail sales dataset from Kaggle.
- **Data Cleaning & Quality Check** – Verify data types, check for missing values and duplicate transaction IDs, and validate sales amount calculations.
- **Data Transformation** – Use Power Query to prepare the dataset and create customer age groups for demographic analysis.
- **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** – Examine revenue trends, product category performance, sales volume, and customer purchasing patterns.
- **KPI Development** – Create DAX measures to calculate total revenue, total transactions, total quantity sold, average transaction value, and total customers.
- **Dashboard Design** – Develop an interactive, single-page dashboard with a consistent color scheme, clear visual hierarchy, and user-friendly slicers.
- **Visualization Development** – Build line, column, horizontal bar, and donut charts to compare sales performance across months, product categories, age groups, and gender.
- **Insight Generation** – Identify revenue patterns, compare sales volume with revenue contribution, and highlight customer segments for further business investigation.


## 📊 Dashboard & Metrics

### 📈 KPI Requirements

- **Total Revenue** – Overall revenue generated from retail transactions.
- **Total Transactions** – Number of unique transactions recorded.
- **Total Quantity Sold** – Total number of units purchased.
- **Average Transaction Value** – Average revenue generated per transaction.
- **Total Customers** – Number of unique customers in the dataset.

### 🔹 Chart Requirements

- **Monthly Revenue Trend** (Line Chart)
- **Revenue by Product Category** (Clustered Column Chart)
- **Revenue by Age Group** (Horizontal Bar Chart)
- **Revenue Share by Gender** (Donut Chart)

### 🎛️ Interactive Features

- **Gender Filter** – Explore sales performance by gender.
- **Product Category Filter** – Focus on selected product categories.
- **Date Range Filter** – Analyse sales performance over a selected period.


## 💡 Key Business Insights

- **Product Category Performance** – Electronics generated the highest total revenue, while Clothing recorded the highest quantity sold.
- **Revenue per Item** – Beauty recorded the highest revenue per item in the analysis, while Clothing recorded the lowest.
- **Customer Gender Analysis** – Female customers contributed approximately 51.06% of total revenue, compared with 48.94% from male customers.
- **Monthly Sales Trend** – May recorded the highest monthly revenue in the dataset, while September recorded the lowest.

These findings are based on the dataset used in this project. Further analysis would be required to determine the underlying reasons behind the observed sales patterns.


## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI** – Dashboard development and data visualization.
- **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation.
- **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)** – KPI measures and analytical calculations.
- **Microsoft Excel / CSV** – Dataset inspection and data preparation.


## 📂 Dataset

The project uses the **Retail Sales Dataset** sourced from Kaggle. The dataset contains 1,000 transactions and customer demographic information, including age and gender, alongside product categories, quantities, unit prices, transaction dates, and total amounts.

The dataset is synthetic and is used for learning, analysis, and portfolio demonstration purposes.

- Apply data cleaning and transformation techniques.
